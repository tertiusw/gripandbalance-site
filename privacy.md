# Grip & Balance — Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 24 October 2026

Grip & Balance is a strength and rehab training log for iPhone, iPad and
Apple Watch. It is designed around a simple rule: **nothing leaves your device
unless you share a file or tap Show me yourself.** Your training data leaves
only when you export it and share the files, which only ever happens when you
ask; tapping *Show me* on an exercise sends that built-in exercise's name to
Google through your browser — never your data (see below).

## What we collect

Nothing. Grip & Balance has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no
third-party SDKs, and no server. The app makes no network connections of its own. Every
workout, routine, and setting you create — including any pain marker you set,
the exercises you tell the app you cannot do, and any workout places you name —
is stored only in the app's
private database on your device, protected with iOS Data Protection (encrypted
whenever your device is locked). Those rules are yours alone: the app never
infers one from your training.

## Apple Health (HealthKit)

If you grant permission, Grip & Balance **reads** your Apple Health workouts to
show the energy you burned during matching training sessions.

- When a session matches an Apple Health workout, the app saves that workout's
  energy figure onto the session's own record — in the app's private database,
  on your device — so your history and charts can show it later without asking
  Health again. Like everything else in that database, it is never transmitted
  anywhere.
- The app never writes to Apple Health, except when you use the Apple Watch
  companion: ending a watch-tracked session saves that workout to Apple Health,
  on your device, under your control.
- Health data is never used for advertising, marketing, or any purpose other
  than showing it to you inside the app.
- You can revoke access at any time in the iOS Settings app under
  Privacy & Security ▸ Health.

## Microphone and speech recognition

After a workout you can record a voice note of up to thirty seconds. The
recording is transcribed by iOS's on-device speech recogniser: the audio is
never written to disk and is discarded as soon as transcription finishes. Only
the resulting text is kept, in that session's notes, exactly as if you had
typed it. If your device cannot transcribe entirely offline, the app does not
record at all — there is no cloud fallback.

iOS's own permission sheet mentions that speech data "will be sent to Apple";
that is Apple's fixed wording, shown for every app. Grip & Balance requests
on-device recognition only. You can revoke microphone and speech access at any
time in iOS Settings under Privacy & Security.

## Location (workout places)

Workout places is off by default. If you turn it on in Settings ▸ Workout
places, the app asks to use your location **while the app is in use** — never in
the background. Each time you open the Workout tab to start a workout, it checks
your location once and compares it, on your device, with the places you have
named, so it can suggest the routines you have done there.

- For each place you name, the app keeps the name and one point at its centre,
  in the app's private database on your device. If you choose not to name a
  place, nothing about it is kept.
- A workout records which of your named places it was started at — not where
  you were. The app keeps no history or trail of your movements.
- Your location is never looked up against a map or address service and is
  never transmitted anywhere — the app has no network access.
- You can rename or delete places, or turn the feature off, in Settings ▸
  Workout places, and revoke location access at any time in iOS Settings under
  Privacy & Security ▸ Location Services.

## Looking up an exercise (Show me)

On a built-in exercise, **Show me** opens a Google image search for that
exercise's name in your own web browser. It happens only when you tap it, and the
search carries the exercise's name and nothing else — none of your sets, weights,
notes, places or profile. Exercises you created and named yourself never offer
it, so your own words never leave this way.

The app still makes no network connection: it hands the address to your browser,
and your browser makes the request. What your browser and Google do with that
search is covered by their privacy policies, not this one. Grip & Balance does
not check the images it finds.

## Coach Mode (training other people)

Coach Mode is off by default. If you turn it on, you can keep separate
profiles for people you train: a display name you choose, their routines and
logged sessions, session notes, and optional markers such as a pain flag. All
of it lives in the same private database on your device, under the same rules
as your own training — no account is created for a client, nothing is synced,
and nothing is transmitted. A client's data leaves the device only when you
yourself share it with the iOS share sheet, and deleting a client's profile
deletes their data.

If you are a practitioner, you remain responsible for your own professional
records — Grip & Balance is not a clinical record.

## Exporting your data

Settings ▸ Export workout history writes your sets (with the name of the place
each workout was started at, if any — never a location), blocks and cardio
segments to CSV files and hands them to the iOS share sheet. Nothing is uploaded: the app
has no network access and no destination of its own. Where those files go —
Files, Mail, another app — is entirely your choice, and once they leave the
share sheet they are outside the app and outside this policy. This is the only
way training data leaves your device.

## Notifications

Rest-timer alerts are local notifications scheduled entirely on your device.
No notification content is sent to any server.

## Data retention and deletion

All data lives on your device and under your control. Deleting the app deletes
its database. There is nothing to delete on our side, because we hold nothing.

## Children

Grip & Balance does not collect personal information from anyone, including
children.

## This website

gripandbalance.com keeps the same promise as the app: it sets no cookies,
runs no analytics, and makes no third-party requests. The web server keeps
ordinary technical logs (as virtually all web servers do); nothing on this
site identifies or profiles you.

## Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app changes how data is handled, this page will be
updated before that version ships, with a new effective date.
